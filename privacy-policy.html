Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

Your Data Stays on Your Machine

AI Roundtable is desktop software that runs locally on your Windows computer. We do not operate servers that collect, store, or process your conversation data.

What We Collect

We collect nothing. The AI Roundtable desktop application does not:

  • Log your questions or AI responses
  • Store your API keys on our servers
  • Track your usage or behavior
  • Use analytics or telemetry
  • Share data with third parties

API Communication

When you use AI Roundtable, your questions are sent directly from your computer to the AI provider you configure (such as OpenRouter, OpenAI, or Google). These providers have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of whichever API provider you choose to use.

Your API Keys

API keys are stored locally on your machine. You bring your own keys. We do not provide, resell, or share API access.

Website

This website (zhaokefei.com) uses Google Analytics for basic traffic measurement. No personal data is collected beyond standard analytics. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or profiling.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at zhaokefei@proton.me.
